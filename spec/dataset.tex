\section*{SolData}
\field{SolDataset.data} is a field-first dictionary: \field{data[field][g]} selects one field of one game.
The integer $g$ is a local game index; \field{data["gameid"][g]} is the original Oracle's Elixir game ID.
All CSV cells remain \textbf{strings}, including numbers, flags, and empty cells (\field{""}).
Numeric conversion, champion encoding, and tensors belong to the model configuration.

\subsection*{Access Forms \normalfont\small--- three value layouts}
\noindent\begin{tabular}{@{}l l l L{6.5cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Scope} & \textbf{Access} & \textbf{Value} & \textbf{Semantics} \\
\midrule
{\color{callblue}Match} & \field{data[f][g]} & \field{str} & One value from the first player row. \\
{\color{movgreen}Player} & \field{data[f][g][p]} & \field{str} & One of ten players, ordered by side and role. \\
{\color{multipurple}Team} & \field{data["team_"+f][g][s]} & \field{str} & Team-row value; $s$ is an integer dictionary key. \\
{\color{markerorange}Draft} & \field{data[f][g][s]} & \field{str} or \field{list[str]} & Special fields below; no \field{team_} prefix. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\subsection*{Indices \normalfont\small--- role order is shared by all player fields}
\noindent\begin{tabular}{@{}l c c c c c@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Side} & \textbf{top} & \textbf{jng} & \textbf{mid} & \textbf{bot} & \textbf{sup} \\
\midrule
Blue ($s=0$) & $p=0$ & $p=1$ & $p=2$ & $p=3$ & $p=4$ \\
Red ($s=1$) & $p=5$ & $p=6$ & $p=7$ & $p=8$ & $p=9$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\smallskip

\noindent $p=5s+r$, with $r \in \{0,1,2,3,4\}$ in the role order above.
Team and draft values use \field{dict[int, ...]} with keys \field{0}, \field{1}; player values use \field{list[str]} of length ten.

\subsection*{Default Fields \normalfont\small--- present after a fresh extraction without extra requests}
\noindent\begin{tabular}{@{}l l L{9.0cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Field} & \textbf{Value at $g$} & \textbf{Semantics} \\
\midrule
\field{gameid} & \field{str} & Original game ID, distinct from local index $g$. \\
\field{date} & \field{str} & Timestamp, typically \field{YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS}; used for splitting. \\
\field{patch} & \field{str} & Patch label, e.g.\ \field{"16.18"}; preserved as text. \\
\field{champion} & \field{list[str]} & Ten champion names in side/role order, not pick order. \\
\field{position} & \field{list[str]} & \field{["top", "jng", "mid", "bot", "sup"] * 2}. \\
\field{result} & \field{list[str]} & Ten player results: \field{"1"} for a win, \field{"0"} for a loss. \\
\field{team_result} & \field{dict[int, str]} & Team outcomes; \field{data["team_result"][g][0]} is Blue's label. \\
\field{firstPick} & \field{dict[int, str]} & Per-side first-pick flag, \field{"1"} or \field{"0"}. Empty cells default to Blue first. \\
\field{pick_order} & \field{dict[int, list[str]]} & Five names per side, copied from the team row's \field{pick1}--\field{pick5}. \\
\field{ban_order} & \field{dict[int, list[str]]} & Five names per side, copied from the team row's \field{ban1}--\field{ban5}. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\smallskip

\noindent \field{REQUIRED_FIELDS} names nine fields; \field{team_result} is also emitted because \field{result} is required.
Pick and ban slots preserve each side's recorded order, not a merged sequence of draft turns.

\section*{CSV Field Catalog}
The following catalog covers the shared 165-column header of the local 2020--2026 CSVs.
Apart from the default fields, columns must be named in \field{requested_fields}.
Spellings, spaces, capitalization, and parentheses are literal.

\subsection*{{\color{callblue}Match Fields} \normalfont\small--- \field{data[f][g]}}
\noindent\begin{tabular}{@{}l L{13.1cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Field} & \textbf{Semantics} \\
\midrule
\field{gameid} & Original game identifier. \\
\field{url} & Source match URL. \\
\field{league} & League or competition label. \\
\field{year} & Dataset year as text. \\
\field{split} & Split or season label. \\
\field{playoffs} & Playoff flag: \field{"1"} or \field{"0"}. \\
\field{date} & Game timestamp, copied from the first player row. \\
\field{game} & Game number within the series; raw cell, possibly empty. \\
\field{patch} & Patch label. \\
\field{datacompleteness} & \field{"complete"} or \field{"partial"}. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\subsection*{{\color{movgreen}Player and Team Fields} \normalfont\small--- request the CSV column name}
For every CSV column $f$ outside the ten match fields and \field{firstPick}, extraction creates
\field{data[f][g]} as a ten-player list. It also creates \field{data["team_"+f][g]} from the two team rows,
except for \field{champion} and \field{position}. Team cells are copied directly; they are not sums of player cells.

\noindent\begin{tabular}{@{}l L{13.1cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Field} & \textbf{Semantics} \\
\midrule
\field{participantid} & Source participant identifier; independent of the zero-based player index $p$. \\
\field{side} & \field{"Blue"} or \field{"Red"}. \\
\field{position} & Player role; no \field{team_position} is produced. \\
\field{playername}, \field{playerid} & Player display name and source ID. Team-row cells may be empty. \\
\field{teamname}, \field{teamid} & Player's team name and source ID. Their team-row keys are \field{team_teamname}, \field{team_teamid}. \\
\field{champion} & Champion name; no \field{team_champion} is produced. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\subsection*{{\color{markerorange}Draft Columns} \normalfont\small--- grouped fields are always available}
\noindent\begin{tabular}{@{}l L{12.2cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Field} & \textbf{Semantics} \\
\midrule
\field{firstPick} & Special side dictionary, never a player list or \field{team_firstPick}. The CSV name is case-sensitive. \\
\field{pick1}, \field{pick2}, \field{pick3}, & Optional raw columns. Requesting one creates a player list and its \field{team_} counterpart. Player cells may be empty. \\
\field{pick4}, \field{pick5} & The default \field{pick_order} already groups the five team cells. \\[3pt]
\field{ban1}, \field{ban2}, \field{ban3}, & Optional raw columns with the same player/team rule. \\
\field{ban4}, \field{ban5} & The default \field{ban_order} groups the five team cells. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\subsection*{Request Rules}
\begin{lstlisting}
data = SolDataset().extract_base_data(requested_fields=["kills", "teamname"]).data
g = next(iter(data["gameid"]))
blue_team = data["team_teamname"][g][0]
blue_kills = int(data["team_kills"][g][0])
\end{lstlisting}
Request \field{"kills"} to obtain both \field{kills} and \field{team_kills}; requesting only \field{"team_kills"} does not load it.
Requests absent from the CSV header do not create populated fields.
The nested \field{defaultdict}s create empty entries on missing lookups: check \field{f in data} and \field{g in data[f]} before reading uncertain keys.

\section*{Statistics}
All fields on this and the next page use the player/team rule:
\field{data[f][g][p]} and \field{data["team_"+f][g][s]}.
Numbers remain strings. Available columns can contain empty cells, including on team rows and for metrics absent in older patches.
